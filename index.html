Everything running under the hood

Your site is two pieces, both built for you alone: a theme branded to your books, and a core plugin written for author sites. Between them they cover everything the site does — so there is no plugin stack to buy, no third-party app to connect, and nothing that breaks when someone else pushes an update. Every setting sits in one dashboard, and the data it collects belongs to you.

Found, and cited

Set up for search engines and for the AI assistants readers now ask.

What authors say

Questions
authors ask

Still stuck on something? Ask us directly — we answer the same day.

Do I own the site when it's done?

Yes. The domain, hosting account, email platform, and files are all in your name. If you leave, nothing breaks.

Can I add a new book myself?

Yes. Book pages are built as a form you fill in — title, blurb, cover, buy links. Your training call covers it, and the recording is yours to keep.

I have a site already. Can you rebuild it?

Often the better option. We migrate your content, keep your existing links working, and carry over your email list.

What do you need from me?

Cover files, your bio, a headshot, retailer links, and any reviews you'd like to feature. If you're missing pieces, we'll help you write them.

Is hosting included?

The first year of hosting is included with every package. After that it's part of the $45/mo care plan, or you can host it yourself.

I haven't published yet. Too early?

No — it's the best time. A pre-launch site collects readers while you finish the book, so release day starts with an audience.
